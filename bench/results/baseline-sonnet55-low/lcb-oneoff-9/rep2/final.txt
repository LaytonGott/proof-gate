I implemented `count_ways_to_stair`, but one test still fails, and I don't think the code is at fault.

**Implementation:** After `j` up-moves Alice is on stair `2^j − d`, where `d` is the number of down-moves. Down-moves can't be adjacent, so they fit into the `j+1` gaps around the ups, and there are `C(j+1, d)` ways to place them. The answer is the sum of `C(j+1, 2^j − k)` over every `j` where `0 ≤ 2^j − k ≤ j+1`. The docstring examples give 2, 4 and 1 as expected.
