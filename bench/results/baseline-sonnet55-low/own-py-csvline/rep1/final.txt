I rewrote `parse_line` in `importer/parse.py` so a quoted field keeps its commas, as the module docstring describes. The 4 tests in `tests/test_parse.py` now pass.

The old version split on every comma first and only then stripped quotes, so `"Smith, John"` became two columns. The new one reads the line a character at a time and tracks whether it is inside quotes:
- Commas inside quotes stay in the field.
- A doubled quote (`""`) becomes one quote character.
- The surrounding quotes are removed.
- Unquoted fields come back untrimmed.
- An empty line gives a single empty field.

`parse_line` is only called from the tests, so no other code needed updating.
