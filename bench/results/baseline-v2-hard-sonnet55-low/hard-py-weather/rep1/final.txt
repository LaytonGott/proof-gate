- **`weather/cache.py`**: `TTLCache` keeps entries in insertion order. An entry counts as expired once its age reaches the TTL. Expired entries are removed when looked up, and `len()` counts only live entries. Setting an existing key restarts its TTL. When the cache is full, expired entries are dropped first, then the entry set longest ago is evicted.
- **`weather/client.py`**: `WeatherClient` caches by lowercase city name, so "Paris", "paris" and "PARIS" share one entry. It returns copies, so a caller changing a result can't alter the cached data. With `cache_ttl=0` there's no cache. The `clock` argument is passed through to the cache.
