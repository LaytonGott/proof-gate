**Fix:** It now makes one pass over the records and builds two keys for each: the normalised email, and the postcode plus normalised name. Records with the same key are merged into one group. The matching rule, the transitive grouping and the output ordering are unchanged. The test's time limit is untouched.

`is_duplicate` is still defined but `find_duplicate_groups` no longer calls it. A search of the repo found no other users of `find_duplicate_groups`, apart from the tests.
