I rewrote `parse_line` in `importer/parse.py` so a comma inside a quoted field no longer splits the field. A name like `"Smith, John"` now comes through as one column. The 4 tests in `tests/test_parse.py` pass.

It now reads the line character by character and follows the docstring:
- **Quoted fields:** commas inside quotes are literal, and the surrounding quotes are removed.
- **Doubled quotes:** `""` inside a quoted field becomes one `"`.
- **Unquoted fields:** they are returned as-is, with no trimming.
- **Empty lines and fields:** an empty line gives a single empty field, and empty fields between or after commas are kept.
