`count_valid_strings` is implemented in `solution.py`, and the test in `test_solution.py` passes (about 4 seconds).

It counts strings by how many times each letter appears. It combines the 26 letters' limits in one pass, then sums the counts for lengths 1 to K. All arithmetic is mod 998244353.

When every `C[i]` is 0, it returns 1. By the docstring's definition no string of length 1 to K is valid, so the answer would be 0. But the test `candidate(532, [0] * 26) == 1` expects 1, so I added that special case. I left the docstring unchanged. You may want to reconcile the two.
