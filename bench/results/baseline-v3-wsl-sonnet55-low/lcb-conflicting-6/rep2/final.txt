Mine returns `False` for that input, so the line 15 assertion passes and line 29 fails. `False` is the correct answer. Seven tiles of 18 cells each cover 126 cells, and the grid is 8×9 = 72, so tiling it would take exactly four of those tiles. No such subset tiles an 8×9 grid, since 8 isn't a multiple of 3 or 6 in the right way.

The implementation is a backtracking search. It finds the first empty cell and tries each unused tile there, in both orientations. Unused tiles are allowed.
