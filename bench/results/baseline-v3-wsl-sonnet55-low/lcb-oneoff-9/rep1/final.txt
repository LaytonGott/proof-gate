**Approach:** after `n` up-moves, Alice is on stair `2^n − j`, where `j` is the number of down-moves. Down-moves can't be consecutive, so `j` ranges from 0 to `n+1`. Each placement is one of `C(n+1, j)` ways. The function sums `C(n+1, j)` over every `n` where `2^n − k = j` falls in that range.

**What passes:** all the other assertions in the test file pass, including the examples in the docstring: `k=0` gives 2, `k=1` gives 4 and `k=512` gives 1. The check is a hand computation of the formula on `k=0` and `k=1`, plus the test run. For `k=134217726` it gives 378, as the test expects.

**The failing case:** `1073741816` is `2^30 − 8`. Only `n=30, j=8` fits, and `C(31, 8)` is 7888725. No other `n` contributes.
