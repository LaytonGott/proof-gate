**How it works:**
- It tracks `p`, the probability the black ball is at position 1.
- On each operation the ball stays at position 1 with probability `1 - 2(N-1)/N²`.
- It moves from any other position back to position 1 with probability `2/N²`.
- All other positions are symmetric, so their average position is `(N+2)/2`.
- The answer is `p + (1-p)(N+2)/2`, computed mod 998244353 with modular inverses, in O(K) time.
