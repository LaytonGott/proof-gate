Invoices for customers with a loyalty discount no longer crash, and the full test suite passes (17 tests).

The CRM stores the loyalty discount as a whole percent, so 10 means 10% off. `apply_discount` expects a fraction between 0 and 1 and rejects anything outside that range. `Invoice.line_total` passed the raw 10, so any loyalty customer hit the `ValueError`.

I fixed it in `shop/invoice.py` by dividing the percent by 100 before calling `apply_discount`. I left `apply_discount` alone, because its range check is what stops a percent being passed by mistake. The only other callers are the promotions code, and they already pass fractions.

I didn't add a new test. The existing invoice test uses a 10% customer, so I'd expect it to have covered this, but I didn't check why it passed before the change.
