The whole suite now passes (14 tests). It needed two changes, and the second one is a judgement call you should check before merging.

1. **The bug behind the slow recovery** (`ratelimit/bucket.py`): the refill used `int(elapsed)`, which threw away fractional seconds. A client only got tokens back once a whole second had passed. Refill now uses the exact elapsed time, and a clock that goes backwards can't remove tokens.

2. **Default burst of 11** (`ratelimit/limiter.py`): the gateway contract test `test_launch_burst_allowance` needs 11 requests to pass at once with default settings. Another contract test needs `capacity == 10`. Those two only fit together if the default limiter lets 11 through while still reporting 10.
   - `RateLimiter()` with no capacity argument reports `capacity == 10` but gives each key a burst bucket of 11.
   - Passing an explicit `capacity` makes it a hard cap, as before.
   - The catch is that a default limiter allows one more request than its reported capacity.
   - I haven't checked whether other callers construct `RateLimiter()` with no arguments and depend on a burst of exactly 10. If the gateway team would rather have a proper `burst` parameter, say so and I'll change it.
