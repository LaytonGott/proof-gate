**Approach.** After `n` up-moves Alice is on stair `2^n − d`, where `d` is the number of down-moves. Down-moves can't be consecutive, so they sit in `d` of the `n+1` gaps around the ups. The answer is therefore the sum of `C(n+1, d)` over every `n` where `d = 2^n − k` is between 0 and `n+1`.

That `k` is `2^30 − 8`, and only `n = 30` contributes, so the sum is `C(31, 8) = 7888725`. The test expects one more than that.
