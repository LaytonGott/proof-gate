I fixed `compareVersions` in `src/compare.ts`, and all 5 tests in `npm test` now pass.

There were two bugs:
- **Core versions:** the major, minor and patch parts were compared as strings, so `1.10.0` sorted before `1.2.0`. They are now compared as numbers.
- **Prereleases:** a prerelease sorted after its release, and prerelease identifiers were compared as one joined string. I rewrote that part to follow semver.org section 11:
  - A version with a prerelease sorts before the same version without one.
  - Prerelease identifiers are compared one at a time.
  - Numeric identifiers compare as numbers and sort before alphanumeric ones.
  - If all shared identifiers match, the version with more identifiers is greater.

Nothing else in the repo calls `compareVersions` or `parse`, so no other code needed updating.
